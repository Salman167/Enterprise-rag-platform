DATA RETENTION POLICY (EU Region)

1. Personal data is retained for a maximum of 365 days unless legal obligations require longer retention.
2. Employees may request data erasure under GDPR Article 17.
3. All document access is logged in the audit trail.
4. Data must remain within EU-approved regions (eu-west-1, eu-central-1).
5. PII must be redacted before logging.
